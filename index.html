About

Hi, I'm Brennan.

I'm an AI/ML research engineer at the Lockheed Martin AI Center and a Yale graduate in computer science and mathematics. I study how learning systems represent the world, and I build the infrastructure that makes them fast enough to study.

My work moves between theory and systems: the geometry of embeddings in reinforcement learning and language models, sample-efficient RL algorithms, and the GPU engineering that turns a week-long experiment into an afternoon.

  • Understand why some representations generalize, by treating them as geometric objects that can be measured.
  • Build RL agents that learn from far fewer samples, drawing on models of human memory.
  • Write clearly about what I learn, including the experiments that didn't work.
